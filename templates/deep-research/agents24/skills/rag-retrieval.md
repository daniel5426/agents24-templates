name: Research Knowledge Retrieval
description: Uses managed organization knowledge as evidence during research and criticism.
tags: [rag, research]
---

Search managed knowledge when it could contain relevant internal, product, or domain evidence. Treat retrieved material as one evidence source: cite its origin when available, compare it with current web evidence when recency matters, and never invent content when the knowledge store is empty or retrieval is irrelevant.
