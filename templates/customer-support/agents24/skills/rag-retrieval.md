name: Grounded Knowledge Retrieval
description: Grounds customer-support answers in retrieved knowledge.
tags: [rag, customer-support]
---

Search the knowledge base before answering product- or company-specific questions. Base claims about products, policies, pricing, procedures, and account behavior on relevant retrieved content. If retrieval returns no relevant content, say that you do not have enough information and do not invent details. You may still answer general questions that do not depend on company-specific information.
